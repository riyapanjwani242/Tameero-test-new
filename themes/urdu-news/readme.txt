== Description ==

Urdu News is a fast, responsive, and elegant WordPress theme crafted specifically for Urdu news portals, newspapers, and editorial publications. Built with full native RTL (Right-to-Left) typography supporting Noto Nastaliq Urdu for editorial headings and Noto Naskh Arabic for comfortable long-form body text. The theme features bidirectional text isolation (bidi-isolation) for seamless display of numbers, English words, dates, and currency exchange rates.

== Features ==

* Homepage Sections:
  - Top Stories Hero: Distinctive 1 + 4 story grid composition for lead editorial reporting.
  - Breaking News Ticker: Horizontally scrolling ticker with pause-on-hover and custom speed control.
  - Financial & Market Rates Strip: Live sample currency exchange (USD/PKR, EUR/PKR, GBP/PKR), gold rates, petroleum rates, and PSX-100 index with trend indicators.
  - Category News Sections: Distinct news blocks with dedicated color-coded accent bars and categories (Siyasat, Khel, Karobar, Technology, Entertainment).
  - Video News Section: Dedicated high-contrast dark band for video reports with thumbnail previews and duration stamps.
  - Tabbed Rail: Interactive vanilla-JS tabs to switch between popular and latest stories with numbered rankings.
  - Opinion & Editorial Section: Columnist cards featuring author avatars, roles, column excerpts, and reading times.
  - Live TV & Stream Strip: 24/7 broadcast player slot with live pulsing indicator and embed support.
  - Image Gallery Stories: Visual photo journalism grid with picture count badges.
  - Authors & Reporters Strip: Horizontal row of author chips highlighting key correspondents and journalists.
  - Newsletter Subscription Card: Conversion-focused newsletter form with clean Urdu microcopy.

* Inner Templates:
  - single.php: Editorial article layout with estimated reading time, author box, social sharing buttons, category tags, and related stories.
  - category.php: Category archive with colored taxonomy header and responsive article grid.
  - archive.php: Multi-purpose archive supporting dates, tags, and custom taxonomies.
  - search.php: RTL-optimized search results template with search query input and result count.
  - page.php: Clean page template for static pages and long-form content.
  - 404.php: User-friendly error template with helpful navigation and search form.
  - sidebar.php: Flexible widgetized sidebar.

* Customizer Options:
  - Colors: Customize primary brand color, dark accent, and background color.
  - Breaking Ticker: Enable/disable toggle, animation speed, and custom ticker items.
  - Section Toggles: Instant on/off switches for all 9 homepage sections (Financial Strip, Category Sections, Video Band, Tabbed Popular/Latest, Opinion Section, Live TV Strip, Image Gallery, Authors Strip, and Newsletter Section).
  - Live TV URL: Embed custom YouTube Live streams or iframe video feeds.
  - AdSense Slots: Pre-configured monetization placements including header billboard (728x90), in-feed article ads, and sidebar banner units.
  - Social Links: Profile link inputs for Facebook, Twitter/X, YouTube, Instagram, and WhatsApp.
  - Footer Text: Editable copyright text and footer biographical description.

* Widget Areas:
  - Main Sidebar (`sidebar-main`): Displayed on article pages and archives.
  - Footer Widget Area (`sidebar-footer`): Multi-column footer widget area.

* Custom Image Sizes:
  - `urdu-hero`: 1200x675 pixels (16:9 aspect ratio, hard-cropped for lead story hero).
  - `urdu-side`: 400x300 pixels (4:3 aspect ratio, hard-cropped for secondary thumbnails and sidebar lists).
  - `urdu-hero-mobile`: 800x450 pixels (16:9 aspect ratio, hard-cropped for mobile viewports).

* SEO & Structured Data:
  - NewsArticle Schema: Automatic JSON-LD Schema.org NewsArticle markup for single articles.
  - Open Graph & Twitter Cards: Dynamic social share meta tags (og:title, og:image, og:description, twitter:card).

== Installation ==

1. In your WordPress admin dashboard, navigate to Appearance > Themes.
2. Click "Add New Theme" and then click "Upload Theme".
3. Select your `urdu-news.zip` file (or upload the `pc-theme-work` directory into `/wp-content/themes/urdu-news/`).
4. Click "Install Now" and wait for the upload to complete.
5. Click "Activate" to activate the Urdu News theme.
6. Configure your primary navigation menu under Appearance > Menus.
7. Add widgets to your sidebar and footer under Appearance > Widgets.

== Customizer Guide ==

To customize your Urdu News website:

1. Open Customizer:
   In your WordPress dashboard, go to Appearance > Customize.

2. Colors (رنگ):
   Navigate to "رنگ (Theme Colors)" to change the primary brand color (default deep blue/navy), accent bar color, or background color.

3. Breaking Ticker (بریکنگ نیوز پٹی):
   Turn the breaking news ticker on or off, adjust ticker scrolling speed, or enter custom breaking headlines.

4. Homepage Section Toggles (صفحہ اول کے سیکشنز):
   Each homepage section has an individual checkbox. You can show or hide any of the following with a single click:
   - Financial Strip (مارکیٹ ریٹس)
   - Category Sections (شعبہ جاتی خبریں)
   - Video News Section (ویڈیو خبریں)
   - Popular / Latest Tabs (مقبول اور تازہ ترین ٹیبز)
   - Opinion Section (کالم و آراء)
   - Live TV Strip (براہِ راست نشریات)
   - Image Gallery (تصویری کہانیاں)
   - Authors Strip (ہمارے نمائندے)
   - Newsletter Section (خبرنامہ)

5. Live TV Settings (براہِ راست نشریات):
   Enter your YouTube Live embed URL or video ID in the "Live TV Embed URL" field to stream live broadcast directly on your homepage.

6. AdSense & Ads (اشتہارات کے مقامات):
   Paste your Google AdSense code snippets or HTML banner tags into the Header Billboard, In-Feed, or Sidebar ad slots.

7. Social Media Links (سوشل میڈیا لنکس):
   Add full URLs to your publication's social profiles (Facebook, Twitter/X, YouTube, Instagram, WhatsApp).

8. Footer Settings (فوٹر کی ترتیبات):
   Enter your copyright notice and footer bio text.

9. Save and Publish:
   Click the "Publish" button at the top of the Customizer to apply all changes live.
